2. What data we collect

Rethink Drinks collects no personal data. Every drink you log is stored only on your iPhone (SwiftData / SQLite). Nothing leaves your device.

We do not collect:

  • names, email addresses, or account information
  • location data
  • advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • analytics or usage data
  • crash reports sent to third parties

3. Processing by Apple

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may collect anonymised download statistics (country, device type). This processing is handled by Apple independently. See apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. Share function

When you share a card (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly), the app uses the native iOS share sheet. What you share and with whom is entirely your choice. We receive no information about this.

5. Your rights under GDPR

Since we process no personal data, rights of access, rectification, and erasure against us generally do not apply. All your data resides exclusively on your device. Deleting the app removes all data permanently.

You still have the right to contact us (see above) with any question. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

6. Data sources for nutrition values

Nutrition values shown in the app come from publicly available scientific sources:

  • Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel (BLS 4.0), Max Rubner Institute, CC BY 4.0
  • USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy (CC0)
  • Manufacturer labels and Open Food Facts

Hydration coefficients for alcoholic beverages are based on Maughan et al. (2015), "Beverage Hydration Index", American Journal of Clinical Nutrition.
